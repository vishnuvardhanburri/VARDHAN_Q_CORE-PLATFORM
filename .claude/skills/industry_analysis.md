# Seven-Industry Quantum Market Analysis Skill

## Scope
Ranking the seven target industries based on quantum exposure and commercial viability.

## Mandatory Checks
- **Traceable Chain**: Every score must be linked to a specific piece of evidence (e.g., "Regulation X requires Y").
- **Consistency**: Use the same scoring model (Severity, Urgency, Access, Budget) for all seven industries.
- **Honest Gaps**: Label missing data as "Unknown" rather than guessing.

## Failure Conditions
- Ranking an industry as "High Priority" without identifying a specific buyer role and a plausible budget.
- Ignoring the "entry barrier" (e.g., procurement cycles in government vs. finance).

## Analysis Workflow
1. **Problem Mapping**: Identify the most critical "long-lived" data in the industry (e.g., 30-year mortgages in banking).
2. **Buyer Identification**: Find the role (e.g., CISO, Head of Crypto-Agility) and their typical KPIs.
3. **Route to Market**: Define the smallest meaningful "Paid Diagnostic" (e.g., "Scan 100 apps for RSA-2048").
4. **Scoring**: Apply the weights and rank.
