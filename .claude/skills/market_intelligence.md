# Competitive and Market Intelligence Skill

## Scope
Researching competitors in the PQC discovery, inventory, and migration space.

## Mandatory Checks
- **Source Verification**: Use dated, verifiable sources. Distinguish between marketing claims and technical evidence.
- **Substitute Analysis**: Analyze not just direct PQC products, but "doing nothing" or "consulting-led" migration.
- **Evidence-Based Comparison**: Compare features based on public API docs, whitepapers, or verified customer case studies.

## Failure Conditions
- Labeling a competitor as "insecure" without specific, evidence-backed technical reasons.
- Inventing a feature for a competitor to make Vardhan look better.
- Treating a partnership announcement as a proven production deployment.

## Intelligence Workflow
1. **Identify**: Find companies offering "Crypto Agility" or "PQC Migration".
2. **Analyze**: Map their capabilities against the Vardhan vertical slice (Discovery $\rightarrow$ Blueprint $\rightarrow$ Auth $\rightarrow$ Verify).
3. **Benchmark**: Contrast their trust model (e.g., "Trust the Vendor") with Vardhan's ("Trust the Evidence").
