## 1. Executive Strategy & Selection Methodology

This campaign structures enterprise outreach across thirty global systemic banking institutions. The target universe is derived authoritatively from the Financial Stability Board (FSB) 2025 Global Systemically Important Bank (G-SIB) register (29 institutions), supplemented by LGT Private Banking to address high-net-worth cross-border digital asset custody workflows.

### Selection & Qualification Gates

Every prospect account is evaluated against five mandatory qualification criteria:

1. **Product Fit:** Concrete vulnerability to transaction tampering, evidence repudiation, cryptographic obsolescence, or cross-entity reconciliation failure across high-value rails.
2. **Corroborated Personas:** Named professionals whose current employer, business unit, and technical or budgetary accountability are verified via primary announcements or professional profiles.
3. **Public Activity Hook:** Dated, verifiable public research, regulatory filings, keynote addresses, or hiring disclosures—never an invented security gap.
4. **Send-Ready Communication:** Individual, lowercase subject line, technical value hook (100–150 words), zero sales friction, offering a working 10-minute demonstration backed by `lab_demo_verify.sh`.
5. **Rigorous Verification Audit:** Clear distinction between verified public facts and unverified operational details. All unconfirmed email addresses and titles are marked `SEND GATE — BLOCKED`.

Swift is designated strictly as an ecosystem infrastructure partner and excluded from the direct bank prospect list.
