**Why clients will pay:** A single DORA/EU AI Act fine can exceed £20M. Vardhan Q-Core costs a fraction of that and provides mathematical proof of compliance that holds up legally.

## 3. UK INNOVATOR FOUNDER VISA STRATEGY

### The Core Plan
Close **2-3 commercial Letter of Intent agreements** before applying.  
Use these letters as proof of commercial traction for UKVI.

### The Discount Lever (Genius Move)
Offer clients a **£1M discount** in exchange for a Letter of Intent:

> *"Our standard enterprise rate is £5M. As our preferred anchor partner supporting our UK establishment, we offer you a preferred partner rate of £4M."*

**Why it works:**
- Client saves real money → their CFO approves it instantly
- You get a signed letter on official corporate letterhead → UKVI sees real commercial demand
- Letter costs the client nothing to write
- 100% legal — it is an *intent*, not a binding contract

### The Order of Operations
1. **Now:** Identify 5-6 potential contacts via LinkedIn / existing network
2. **Week 1-2:** Reconnect casually, introduce Vardhan Q-Core
3. **Week 3-4:** Demo the platform, propose the partnership + discount offer

5. **Week 7+:** Submit UK Innovator Founder Visa application with letters
